Of 271 job seekers who'd had an interview, 100 say employers often go silent afterwards.

Not after the application. After the interview.

That's from Huntr's survey of job seekers in October 2026.

You prepare for days.
You research the company.
You take the morning off.
You send the thank-you email.

Then nothing.

Silence costs a company nothing. It costs the candidate weeks of hoping.

Two lines would fix it:
"We've gone with someone else. Here's one thing that would have made the difference."

Recruiters, what stops that email being sent?

Tailored CVs were marked interviewed 1.85 times as often as untailored ones.

Nearly 2 million applications, October 2024 to June 2026, tracked by Huntr.

So why doesn't everyone tailor?

44% of job seekers say it takes too long.
40% don't think it makes a difference.

Here is the ten-minute version:

1. Copy the five skills the advert repeats most.
2. Use those exact words in your top three bullet points.
3. Move your most relevant experience to the top, even if it isn't the newest.

Same experience. Written for the job in front of you.

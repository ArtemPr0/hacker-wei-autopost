People who sent 11 to 20 applications recorded an interview 8.1% of the time.

People who sent 100 or more: 2.9%.

That's from Huntr's job search data for April to June 2026.

More applications. Fewer interviews for each one.

Huntr is clear this doesn't prove that sending fewer causes more interviews.

But think about what usually happens around application 60.

You stop reading the advert properly.
You stop changing the CV.
You start just pressing apply.

Of 1,554 people who got their first offer, 61% got it within 50 applications.

Volume feels like progress. It rarely is.

What number are you on right now?
